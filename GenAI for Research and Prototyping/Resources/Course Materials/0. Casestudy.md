# Case Study: Switzerland's Future Energy Mix

**ZHAW Summer School — GenAI for Research and Prototyping**

*Time: no more than 1.5 hours · Work in small teams*

---

## Your task

You are part of the Swiss energy agency. Your task is to plan the future types of energy
sources that will be used in Switzerland in the next 10 years.

Draft a report that answers the following question:

> **Which energy source, or mix of energy sources, should Switzerland invest in?**

---

## Aspects to consider

Your report has to consider the following aspects:

- Cost
- Environmental impact
- Reliability
- Scalability
- Implementation time
- Land use
- Public acceptance
- Energy security

---

## Suggested starting sources

Ground your research in official Swiss sources before turning to secondary material:

- **[Energy Perspectives 2050+ storymap](https://www.uvek-gis.admin.ch/BFE/storymaps/AP_Energieperspektiven/index.html?lang=de&selectedSzenario=ZB&selectedSektor=ALL&selectedDimension=ET&selectedFly=01)** (BFE/UVEK-GIS) — interactive tool for exploring the federal *Energieperspektiven 2050+* scenarios by energy carrier, end use, and sector through 2050. Switch scenarios (e.g. `ZB` = Zero Basis / net-zero) and sectors to compare projected energy mixes.
- **[Energiestrategie 2050](https://www.bfe.admin.ch/de/energiestrategie2050)** (BFE) — the official overview of Switzerland's Energy Strategy 2050: legal basis (revised Energy Law, approved by referendum 21 May 2017), goals, and ongoing monitoring reports on implementation progress.

---

## Deliverable

Draft a report that:

- Recommends an energy source or mix of energy sources for Switzerland.
- Considers each of the aspects listed above.
- Covers **policy recommendations**.
