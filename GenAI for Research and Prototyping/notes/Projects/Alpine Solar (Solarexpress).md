# Alpine Solar (Solarexpress)

Why "just build more solar" isn't the whole story — where it's built matters as much as how
much.

- **What:** the fast-track permitting regime for large ground-mounted/alpine PV plants,
  extended through the transition period by [[Mantelerlass 2024]].
- **Why alpine sites matter:** above the winter fog layer, with snow reflection boosting
  output, alpine PV can generate roughly half its annual output in winter months —
  disproportionately valuable for closing the [[Winter Import Gap]] even though it's a small
  fraction of total [[Solar PV]] capacity.
- **Cost:** costs more per kWh than lowland Plateau PV (terrain, snow-load engineering, short
  construction seasons), but that extra cost buys winter value LCOE alone doesn't capture.
- **Acceptance:** roughly half of planned Solarexpress-type projects have been approved where
  put to a cantonal/communal vote — achievable with local buy-in and revenue sharing, but not
  automatic (some cantons have rejected accelerated planning procedures for solar).
