# Grimsel 4 Pumped Storage

A concrete, dated example of what "hydro flexibility" actually looks like in practice.

- **What:** a pumped-storage retrofit at Grimsel, ~150 MW, CHF ~250 million.
- **Timeline:** permit filed late 2024, commissioning ~2031–2032 — the real-world data point
  behind [[Hydropower]]'s ~7–8 years permit-to-commissioning implementation time.
- **Why it matters:** buys flexibility and storage, not just energy — it reuses an existing
  reservoir, so its incremental land-use and environmental impact is markedly lower than a
  greenfield hydro project.
- **Economic framing:** its value should be judged against balancing/import-avoidance costs,
  not plain LCOE — see [[Cost Comparison]].
