# Mantelerlass 2024

The single most important recent legal change, and the baseline every other note in this
vault treats as binding.

- **Full name:** Federal Act on a Secure Electricity Supply with Renewable Energies
  ("Mantelerlass" / Umbrella Act; also called the Stromgesetz).
- **Status:** approved by **68.7% of voters on 9 June 2024**, in force since
  **1 January 2025**.
- **Binding renewables target:** 35 TWh/year of new renewable electricity (solar, wind,
  biomass, small hydro) by 2035, versus ~6 TWh/year in 2022 — roughly a six-fold increase.
  Drives [[Solar PV]] and [[Wind Power]] buildout above all.
- **Winter import cap:** caps maximum net winter electricity imports at **5 TWh** — the
  binding constraint behind [[Winter Import Gap]].
- **Permitting reform:** designates renewable projects "of national interest"; the
  2025/2026 follow-up "acceleration law" (in force from 1 April 2026) requires cantons to set
  up a single consolidated approval process for large solar and wind projects.
- **Solarexpress:** extends the fast-track for large ground-mounted/alpine PV plants through
  the transition period — see [[Alpine Solar (Solarexpress)]].

[[Recommended Portfolio]] treats this act as the baseline scenario to *accelerate*, not a
starting point to reargue from zero.
