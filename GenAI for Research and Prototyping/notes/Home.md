# Switzerland's Future Energy Mix — Vault Home

This folder (`GenAI for Research and Prototyping/`) is ready to open directly as an
**Obsidian vault** — it's the live-demo material for the workshop's Obsidian demonstration
(see [`../workshop-guide.md`](../workshop-guide.md), Lecture Block 1).

> **To open it:** in Obsidian, choose *Open folder as vault* and select the
> `GenAI for Research and Prototyping` folder itself (not just `notes/`) — the atomic notes
> below link across into the original research notes and report in
> [`../solution/`](../solution/), and Obsidian resolves `[[wikilinks]]` vault-wide regardless
> of subfolder.

## How this vault is organized

- **[[Case Study Brief]]** — the student-facing assignment.
- **[[Recommended Portfolio]]** — the report's recommendation and priority table.
- **Sources/** — one atomic note per energy source (entity notes), each summarizing its
  profile across the case study's eight aspects and linking out to the relevant themes,
  projects, and policy notes.
- **Themes/** — cross-cutting atomic notes for the ideas that connect multiple sources: the
  winter import gap, the nuclear decommissioning timeline, the Mantelerlass, the nuclear
  referendum.
- **Projects/** — two concrete projects (Grimsel 4, Solarexpress alpine solar) that show up
  across several aspects and make good graph-view hubs.
- The six original **Research Notes** (`solution/docs/01-…` to `06-…`) and the full
  **[report](../solution/report.md)** carry the sourced detail and citations — every note
  here links back to them rather than re-stating citations.

## Try it live (Lecture Block 1 demo)

1. Open **[[Winter Import Gap]]** and **[[Swiss Nuclear Decommissioning Timeline]]** side by
   side — note how each links to the other, to [[Nuclear Power]], and to
   [[Mantelerlass 2024]].
2. Open **Graph View** and filter by the `#energy-source` tag to see the six sources cluster
   around the shared policy and theme notes.
3. Click through [[Nuclear Power]] → [[Swiss Nuclear Ban Referendum]] →
   [[Policy and Legal Context]] to show students how one click-path replaces several paragraphs
   of cross-referencing prose — the point of atomic notes.
