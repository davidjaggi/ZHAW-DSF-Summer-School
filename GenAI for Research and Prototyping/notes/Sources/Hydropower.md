# Hydropower

Switzerland's backbone source and its single most valuable reliability asset.

- **Current role:** ~55–60% of generation (large + small, incl. pumped storage) — see
  [[Current Energy Mix]].
- **Cost:** existing large hydro is an effectively sunk asset with very low marginal cost —
  the cheapest electricity Switzerland already has. New-build hydro runs ~$0.057/kWh
  globally; new investment here is mostly efficiency/flexibility retrofits like
  [[Grimsel 4 Pumped Storage]], not new volume.
- **Reliability / energy security:** dispatchable on demand, reservoirs can be timed to
  release in winter — the most valuable lever for closing the [[Winter Import Gap]].
  Constrained by reservoir levels (prior snowmelt/rainfall) and an already largely built-out
  resource.
- **Land use:** highest footprint per unit of *new* output, but reservoirs are historic,
  multi-purpose infrastructure (flood control, tourism, irrigation) already built — almost no
  new large sites remain.
- **Public acceptance:** generally the most broadly accepted source (long history, "Swiss
  identity" association), though new projects still face fish-migration and
  landscape-protection objections.
- **Environmental impact:** near-zero operational emissions; existing reservoirs already
  altered river ecology decades ago — a sunk cost for existing plants, but a hard constraint
  on new large hydro. Small-hydro/pumped-storage retrofits have markedly lower incremental
  impact.
- **Scalability:** low-to-moderate — growth is in flexibility, not new volume.
- **Implementation time:** ~7–8 years permit-to-commissioning for a retrofit like
  [[Grimsel 4 Pumped Storage]].
