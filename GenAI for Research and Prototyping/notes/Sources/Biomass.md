# Biomass

A small, steady contributor with a low, largely fixed ceiling.

- **Current role:** part of the ~5% "other renewables" share (biomass, biogas, waste-to-energy)
  alongside small hydro; sustainable domestic potential is only ~50% exploited.
- **Cost / environmental impact:** can be near-carbon-neutral if sustainably sourced
  (forestry residues); imported biomass (e.g. wood pellets) raises supply-chain and
  true-carbon-neutrality questions.
- **Scalability:** a low absolute ceiling — ~10% of primary energy at full exploitation,
  already roughly half used.
- **Reliability:** a steady, dispatchable-ish small contributor — used as a fixed input in the
  seasonal winter-balance calculation behind [[Winter Import Gap]].
