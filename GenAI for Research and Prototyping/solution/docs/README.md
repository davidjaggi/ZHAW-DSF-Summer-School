## Methodology

Research was gathered via targeted web search against reputable primary and secondary sources: the Swiss Federal Office of Energy (BFE/SFOE), Swissgrid, ElCom, IEA, IRENA, ETH Zurich's Energy Science Center, and established energy-sector press (SWI swissinfo.ch, World Nuclear News, Water Power Magazine) — in line with the case study's Task 1 instruction to use "government reports, academic papers, and industry publications." Every note ends with a **Sources** section linking the specific pages consulted. Figures are current as of **August 2026**; Switzerland's energy policy (especially the nuclear-ban referendum) is actively moving, so treat contested/near-term figures as a snapshot, not a permanent fact — the notes flag where this applies (e.g., the pending nuclear referendum outcome).

Where no Switzerland-specific figure exists in public sources at the needed granularity (e.g., LCOE, land-use intensity), global benchmark data is used and explicitly adjusted with Swiss-specific qualitative context rather than presented as a precise domestic number.
