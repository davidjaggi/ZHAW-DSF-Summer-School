# GenAI for Research and Prototyping

Students at the ZHAW Summer School should solve a case-study to gain knowledge on the use of Generative AI in research and prototyping. The case-study should prepare them to solve one of our real-world problems suggested by our partners. The case-study should take no more than 2 hours to complete.

See [`workshop-guide.md`](workshop-guide.md) for the full facilitator guide (timetable, lecture content, prompting protocol, and reference Streamlit code) behind this case study.

## Case Study

You are part of switzerlands the swiss energy agency. Your task is to plan the the future types of energy sources that will be used in Switzerland in the next 10 years. Draft a report and an interactive dashboard that asnwers the following question:

**Which energy source or mix of energy sources should Switzerland invest in?**

You have to consider the following aspects:
 - Cost
 - Environmental impact
 - Reliability
 - Scalability
 - Impelmentation time
 - Land use
 - Public acceptance
 - Energy security

Furthermore, the report sould cover policy recommendations.

### Task 1

Research and gather data on the current energy sources used in Switzerland, their costs, environmental impacts, reliability, scalability, implementation time, land use, public acceptance, and energy security. Use reputable sources such as government reports, academic papers, and industry publications.

### Task 2

Analyze the data and identify trends, strengths, and weaknesses of each energy source. Consider how these factors may change over the next 10 years based on technological advancements, policy changes, and market dynamics.

### Task 3

Create an interactive dashboard that visualizes the analyzed data and allows users to explore different scenarios for energy investment in Switzerland.

### Task 4

Draft a comprehensive report that summarizes the findings and provides policy recommendations for Switzerland's energy future.
